We hold a block of rooms from Wednesday 2nd December through to Tuesday 8th December 2026 with the following rates:

Standard room

  • Single occupancy with buffet breakfast - €120.00 per night per room, incl VAT (6%).
  • Doubles and twins with buffet breakfast - €136.00 per night per room, incl VAT. (6%)

City Taxes are not included and will be charged on arrival, at the amount in force of €4 per night/person, according to the regulations of the Municipality of Lisbon. Conditions are valid for SMOFcon participants, for stays between Wednesday 2nd December to 8th December 2026 (maximum 6 nights). Any other day or regular room types can be reserved separately through the website, without the code. Wifi is available free throughout the hotel.

Note that there are a very limited number of accessible rooms in the hotel, along with a number of suites. These are not bookable directly with the hotel. For accessible rooms we will prioritise members who have indicated accessibility needs when joining. If interested, please contact us at info@smofconlisbon.org

To book

  • Please go to the hotel website VIP Executive Arts Hotel, choose your dates and use the Promotional code: SMOFCON
  • Note that booking without the code will result in the prevailing availability, market price and conditions being offered. There could be a small number of promotional rates available which are better than the official convention rate.

Payment, Cancellation & Reimbursement policies

  • Payment of the total stay (100%) upon reservation. The first night amount is not subject to any refund
  • Deadline for reservation: 1st October 2026
  • Cancellations until 2nd October 2026: the hotel returns all value with the exception of that relating to the first night.
  • Cancellations after 3rd October 2026: No refunds will be made

Parking

Parking is available at the hotel for those attending the convention at a cost of €10 per day, payable at the check in or in the automatic machines.

Alternative accommodation

There are several other hotels nearby and across the city, which might suit your plans for the convention or wider travel. Examples nearby include the Moxy Lisboa Oriente, Olissippo Oriente, and Ibis Lisboa Parque das Naçoes.

Any problems...

For all queries or difficulties in booking, please let us know using info@smofconlisbon.org
